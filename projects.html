Robotics · In progress

Terra Labs: Cove

As Electronics Co-Lead, I help integrate a seven-degree-of-freedom autonomous robotic arm built around a Teensy 4.1, Raspberry Pi 5, and CAN bus actuator network. My work spans embedded communication, hardware bring-up, actuator interfaces, power distribution, and the safety boundary between low-level firmware and ROS 2 control.

Audio engineering

Custom high-fidelity speaker

I designed and built a Bluetooth speaker from first principles: enclosure modeling, driver selection, analog crossover design, soldering, wiring, and assembly. Acoustic testing with signal generators and oscilloscopes closed the loop between the calculations and what the finished system actually produced.

  • Analog circuits
  • Acoustics
  • Fabrication

Machine learning · Audio

Unconditional audio generation

I built a diffusion model that learns from the spectrogram of a single piece of music and generates new audio material in its sonic vocabulary. Training on one source at a time keeps the compute requirement low enough for common consumer GPUs while preserving a distinct musical character.

  • Diffusion
  • PyTorch
  • Spectrograms
